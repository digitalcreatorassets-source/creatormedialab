Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy covers Creator Media Lab's internal automation tools ("Herald" and related WORKHORSE studio systems), specifically the Pinterest API integration used to schedule and publish pins to our own Pinterest business account.

1. Information We Collect

  • Pinterest OAuth tokens: when we connect our Pinterest business account, Pinterest issues us an access token and refresh token via its official OAuth 2.0 flow. We never see or store your Pinterest password.
  • Board and pin identifiers: the IDs of our own Pinterest boards and the pins we publish, used to schedule posts and avoid duplicate pins.
  • Locally generated content: captions, titles, and images our internal tools generate before publishing them as pins.

2. How We Use Information

This data is used exclusively to authenticate with Pinterest's API and publish scheduled pins to our own account's boards. We do not access, read, or store data belonging to any other Pinterest user or account.

3. Content Compliance

All content published through this integration is automatically screened against Pinterest's Community Guidelines before posting, including a strict filter that blocks any adult, sexually explicit, or sexually suggestive content, nudity, or promotion of commercial sexual services. Only general-audience, brand-safe content (such as photography tips, tutorials, and business updates) is eligible for publishing through this integration.

4. Data Storage & Security

OAuth tokens and related data are stored locally on our own private infrastructure, are never sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party other than Pinterest's API itself, and are not used for advertising or tracking purposes.

5. Data Retention

Tokens and associated data are retained only for as long as the Pinterest integration remains connected. If we disconnect the integration or revoke access, associated tokens are deleted.

6. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to digitalcreatorassets@gmail.com.
